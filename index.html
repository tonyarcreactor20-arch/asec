Guide 01

How to Prepare Footage for Editing

A simple checklist before sending your video files.

Read Article
Organize your video clips into folders. Send the original footage at its best available quality. Include your preferred format, aspect ratio, reference videos, music preferences and any required text. Mention the deadline before editing begins.
Guide 02

What Makes a Good Instagram Reel?

Plan your content for a clearer and more engaging video.

Read Article
Start with a clear idea. Use an engaging opening, readable captions, suitable music and purposeful cuts. Keep the video focused on one message and use a vertical format when the content is intended for Reels. Good editing supports a strong idea; it cannot guarantee views or viral reach.
Guide 03

How to Request a Custom Editing Quote

Help clients share the details needed for an accurate price.

Read Article
Share the video type, raw footage duration, desired final length, reference link, subtitles or effects required and delivery deadline. The editor can then estimate the work and confirm the price before the project starts.
